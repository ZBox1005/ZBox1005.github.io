I am a 2nd year CS Ph.D. student at [Rutgers University](https://www.rutgers.edu/), advised by [Prof. Ruixiang (Ryan) Tang](https://www.ruixiangtang.net/). Before Rutgers, I completed my master's and bachelor's degrees at the [SIGMA Lab](http://sigma.whu.edu.cn/) of [Wuhan University](https://www.whu.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Zengmao Wang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=tTqiJpQAAAAJ&hl=en-US) and [Prof. Bo Du](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Shy1gnMAAAAJ&hl=en-US).

My research focuses on **trustworthy agentic AI** and **LLM post-training and reasoning**. I study how to audit autonomous agents as they act, predict failures early, and make an LLM's confidence better reflect the correctness of its reasoning. I also work on **reliable visual perception**, including out-of-distribution detection and distinguishing AI-generated images from real ones, and explore ML applications in healthcare such as immunology and cell morphology.
